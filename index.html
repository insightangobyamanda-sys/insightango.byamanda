What is Insightango?

An immersion into
the real Buenos Aires

This isn’t a tour package. Insightango is a curated, deeply personal journey designed to show you the authentic soul of Argentina. Our approach goes beyond sightseeing, combining the art of improvisation, the true heartbeat of social tango, with an exclusive look at local life.

Skip the usual routes. We’ll explore the dynamic city center and venture into the non-commercial neighborhood milongas that most visitors never find. We’ll wander through the historic streets where tango was born and sit at tables where history still lingers in the air.

Every journey is tailored to your pace. Whether we’re discovering hidden city gems, sharing iconic flavors, or heading to the countryside for an authentic asado, every detail is handpicked with a local’s insight.

The Experiences

The Hidden Milonga

Amanda takes you to the milongas that locals keep to themselves — neighbourhood dance halls far from the tourist circuits. A space to observe the tradition or join the dance floor, immersing yourself in the local codes and the true culture of the embrace.

Tango & Local Nightlife
♦

Tango City Tour

Explore the legendary corners of Boedo, Pompeya, Barracas, and Almagro. Guided by a specialized historian and hosted by Amanda, we’ll visit the cafes that inspired tango’s greatest lyrics. The journey ends sharing coffee and conversation at a century-old confectionery.

History & Heritage
♦

Tango Dance Class

Learn the foundations or refine your style in private sessions. Guided by Amanda’s specialized eye, you will build confidence and unlock the unique dancer within you. To enrich your journey, Amanda invites you into semi-private classes with her own local students, creating a warm circle of friends to hit the milongas together.

Dance & Interpretation
♦

A Day in the Countryside

Leave the city for a scenic drive to the heart of the province. Enjoy an asado in century-old general stores, explore artisan fairs where the gaucho tradition is alive, and join our monthly festivals of folk music and dance.

Rural & Tradition
♦

Literary & Spanish Sessions

Explore Argentina’s soul through its great authors and poets. Private Spanish lessons and literary talks led by a literature teacher. From coffee shop sessions to visits at the National Library or writers’ historic houses. A drive through Buenos Aires' words.

Café & Books
♦

Porteño Table

A curated culinary journey of iconic neighborhood spots, including classic bodegones, parrillas, and pizzerias. Experience the city that never sleeps through its authentic flavors, enjoying a local gastronomy that stays alive long after midnight.

Flavor & Identity
♦

Tango Show Night

Enjoy an elegant three-course dinner followed by a spectacular live performance with an orchestra, period costumes, and world-class dancers staging the evolution of tango—often featuring traditional folklore.

Stage & Flashback
♦

Teatro Colón Night

Attend one of the world's finest opera, ballet, and music houses. Amanda secures the best locations and accompanies you to the performance, followed by a late-night dinner—a true local custom—to round off a monumental evening.

Art & Performance

An intimate evening of high-caliber live tango, shared among local friends over homemade food and wine. The heart of it all

The Milonga.
As it really is.

A real Buenos Aires milonga

The milonga is where tango truly happens—not a stage, but a daily social ritual. This is an unmasked, everyday space where nothing is prepared for the tourist; everything is built by and for the locals. Amanda guides you into these friendly neighborhood environments where people gather to dance, look at each other, and connect, rather than just show off.

Because you are entering a real, living culture, you don't stay an outsider—you become one of the locals, welcomed into their conversations, sharing tables with regulars, and mingling with Amanda’s own Buenos Aires students. It is an open invitation to experience the warmth of a real embrace, the look, and the true camaraderie of the dance floor.

Your guide

Amanda Berrueco

Amanda is a professional dancer, choreographer, and educator with over 20 years of experience. Born and raised immersed in Argentine culture, she holds a University Degree in Choreographic Composition from the UNA (National University of the Arts) in Buenos Aires.

Having danced since childhood and grown up consuming the city’s highest cultural and artistic expressions, she offers a highly sophisticated perspective on Buenos Aires. She has spent decades in the authentic milonga circuit as a true milonguera. This urban background is enriched by her deep family roots in the Argentine countryside, giving her a firsthand understanding of rural customs and folklore.

For over a decade, Amanda has been designing highly personalized itineraries for international visitors. In addition to these tailored immersions, she has spent the last 7 years teaching tango and body alignment virtual classes to a dedicated global community.

All experiences conducted in English.

After briefly meeting Amanda in person in Buenos Aires back in 2020, I started taking Zoom classes with her. Four years later, my first full in-person immersion with Amanda was in November 2024. She picked me up at the airport, took me to my apartment (which included a dance studio!), showed me around my Palermo-Soho neighborhood, and answered all my questions about living in this exciting new city. Together, we took in the opera at Teatro Colon, the traditional asados and fairs in the Buenos Aires countryside, and wonderful tango shows in this sophisticated city. Best of all, she had organized an immersive program of rigorous private lessons, exhilarating milongas, including one very communal, open-air street tango festival with a live band! In these almost daily private lessons, she reinforced the tango and milonga skills I had been learning in her Zoom classes. And she ratcheted up those skills, showed me the finer points of technique, and the cultural subtleties I'd need in order to really fit into the porteño tango and milonga world! It wasn't just about learning new steps—although she certainly taught me those!—but also educating me to the attitudes, the "character" of each orchestra, each song. She helped me tap into the passion of this beautiful music. She taught me to translate that passion into the "cells of motion" of tanguero and milonguero styles of dancing. Even how to start the evening with authentic Argentine cabeceos! Her aim was very ambitious, and she made me feel it too! I wanted "to be the Tango, and not just dance tango steps!" And I knew, under her instruction and with a lot of hard work, I could achieve that ambition. I knew I'd have to really work at this tanguero and milonguero character every waking hour, or I'd lose it. Because tango is life. At those inimitable milongas porteñas, I was no longer shy; I had the time of my life! From the first cabeceo to the last entrancing dance with one of those charming bailarinas or with the captivating Amanda herself, I enjoyed "the party," the feast of life as I never had before. Those three weeks went by too fast! It was hard to say good-bye to the warm hospitality shown me by Amanda and her beautiful family and friends. That's why I returned in 2025 with a friend, and why I continue training via Zoom year-round. Now, six years after our journey began, I am already preparing for my next annual immersion in 2026: an incredible 47-day stay!
